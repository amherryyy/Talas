# Duno live-demo runbook

The owner reports that the course requires a live demo and has no separate Phase 2 rubric. This outline follows the approved Phase 1 scope. No demo duration or scoring criteria were provided, so it can be shortened to fit the instructor's schedule.

## Before presenting

- Open the hosted HTTPS site and confirm the homepage and sign-in page load.
- Keep a local XAMPP copy available as a live fallback if hosting or internet access fails.
- Use synthetic student and admin accounts only. Keep passwords in a password manager or private notes, never in this document or on screen.
- Prepare a few synthetic subjects, topics, a saved plan, and a saved session so the demo does not depend on waiting for a timer.
- Choose a synthetic student account with some saved study time so you can show its level and unlocked Aralytics extras. The dashboard check-in prompt may appear; complete it only with synthetic ratings or dismiss it.
- Close unrelated tabs and hide browser password prompts, bookmarks, and private account details.
- Test student and admin access separately before the presentation. Do not use the admin account to create or expose student-specific records.

## Suggested walkthrough

1. **Start as a visitor:** show the Duno homepage and the Home, Tools, Guide, and Join navigation. Open the public Guide section.
2. **Show the student workflow:** sign in with the synthetic student account and show the dashboard mascot and study level. If the optional check-in prompt appears, explain its private, optional purpose and dismiss or submit it using synthetic ratings. Open Duno Focus and explain how its priority list reflects the student's topic details. Use one search or filter example.
3. **Plan study time:** open Duno Plan, enter a short available-time window, generate the plan, and show how the plan stays within that limit. Show an existing saved plan or make one small adjustment.
4. **Record a session:** open Duno Timer and point out the mascot and suggested 25/5 or 50/10 focus rhythms. Show the subject/topic selection and explicit save behavior, then use a prepared saved session to demonstrate the resulting record without waiting through a full timer.
5. **Review Aralytics:** show the descriptive study summary and print preview. If the prepared student has reached a milestone, show its unlocked insight and explain that core Aralytics remains open at every level. Keep private check-in details off the projector unless deliberately showing a synthetic entry.
6. **Show the admin boundary:** sign in with the separate synthetic admin account and show aggregate totals only. If appropriate, sign back in as the student and show that the admin route is denied.
7. **Close cleanly:** sign out and return to the public homepage.

## Keep the demo accurate

- Describe priority, planning, and summaries as deterministic rules based on entered data. Do not imply grade prediction or guaranteed learning outcomes.
- Do not describe the app as using AI recommendations; Duno's study suggestions are curated content.
- Do not show `.env`, hosting credentials, database credentials, real student records, or password fields containing saved secrets.
- Keep the optional workload and energy check-in framed as a private self-reflection tool, not a health assessment.
- The live host's `/.env` response was not verified as an HTTP 403/404; do not claim otherwise.
