# Duno brand identity

## Approved identity

- **Wordmark:** `duno.` (lowercase, including the period)
- **Tagline:** `Your Little Study Buddy.`
- **Mascot:** a friendly Eureka lightbulb wearing a traditional Filipino salakot.
- **Product areas:** Duno Focus, Duno Plan, Duno Guide, and Duno Timer.
- **Existing feature:** Aralytics. Keep this name.

## Color tokens

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purple | `#7465D8` | Main brand color and primary actions |
| Mint | `#B9E8D1` | Supportive highlights and calm success surfaces |
| Gold | `#FFDA92` | Small warm accents and mascot details |
| Lavender | `#EDE9FE` | Soft brand panels and secondary highlights |
| Canvas | `#F7F6FC` | Main page background |
| Text | `#2E2E3A` | Main text on light surfaces |

Use a dark, high-contrast text color with the palette. Keep keyboard focus visible and honor reduced-motion preferences.

## Mascot artwork and use

The owner supplied a brand board on 2026-10-19 and approved its Duno mascot direction for interim app use: a cheerful yellow lightbulb wearing a Filipino salakot, with purple study accessories. A transparent static PNG adaptation is stored at `public/assets/images/duno-mascot.png` and used on the public welcome page and student dashboard. The supplied board is the visual reference; the PNG is an interim app asset rather than a final vector/logo export.

Keep mascot use friendly and restrained. It is decorative and must not carry essential information. The owner approved wider on-screen use and subtle animation of the existing static PNG on 2026-10-25, then requested more animation and interaction on the login/register pages on 2026-10-27. Those pages now let students tap, click, or keyboard-activate Duno for a brief bounce and an encouraging message. The board proposes **happy**, **thinking**, **focused**, **celebrating**, and **resting** states; add those as separate approved assets only when the owner asks for the expanded set. A final transparent vector/logo export can replace the interim raster asset later. Honor reduced-motion preferences if motion is added.

## User-facing naming

Use `duno.` and its tagline in shared brand areas. Use the four product-area names exactly as listed above. Retain **Aralytics**. The `Talas` folder name and historical proposal references may remain for repository compatibility and project history; do not show Talas in new or rebranded UI. Do not rename database tables, migrations, routes, or stored identifiers as part of the visual rebrand.
