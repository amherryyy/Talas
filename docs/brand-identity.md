# Duno brand identity

## Approved identity

- **Wordmark:** `duno.` (lowercase, including the period)
- **Tagline:** `Your Little Study Buddy.`
- **Mascot:** a friendly Eureka lightbulb wearing a traditional Filipino salakot.
- **Product areas:** Duno Focus, Duno Plan, Duno Guide, and Duno Timer.
- **Existing feature:** Aralytics. Keep this name.

## Color tokens

| Token | Hex | Use |

| Purple | `#7465D8` | Main brand color and primary actions |
| Mint | `#B9E8D1` | Supportive highlights and calm success surfaces |
| Gold | `#FFDA92` | Small warm accents and mascot details |

| Canvas | `#F7F6FC` | Main page background |
| Text | `#2E2E3A` | Main text on light surfaces |

Use a dark, high-contrast text color with the palette. Keep keyboard focus visible and honor reduced-motion preferences.

## Mascot artwork and use

The owner supplied a brand board on 2026-10-19 and approved its Duno mascot direction for interim app use: a cheerful yellow lightbulb wearing a Filipino salakot, with purple study accessories. The supplied board is the visual reference; `public/assets/images/duno-mascot.png` remains the original transparent artwork, not a final vector/logo export. The app now uses small transparent WebP derivatives in `public/assets/images/`: `duno-mascot-welcome.webp` for welcome and routine-success states, `duno-mascot-curious.webp` for information, `duno-mascot-alert.webp` for warnings, `duno-mascot-support.webp` for errors and check-ins, and `duno-mascot-celebrating.webp` for rewards and the activated encouragement interaction.

Keep mascot use friendly and restrained. It is decorative and must not carry essential information. The owner approved wider on-screen use and subtle animation on 2026-10-25, then requested more animation and interaction on the login/register pages on 2026-10-27. Those pages let students tap, click, or keyboard-activate Duno for a brief bounce and an encouraging message; the activated interaction briefly swaps to the celebration artwork. On 2026-10-29, the owner requested expression assets for welcome, motivational, and notification moments. The board's **happy**, **thinking**, **celebrating**, and **resting** ideas informed the current derivatives; focused and resting artwork has not been created. Preserve the original PNG as the source image. A final transparent vector/logo export can replace the interim raster artwork later. Honor reduced-motion preferences if motion is added.

## User-facing naming

Use `duno.` and its tagline in shared brand areas. Use the four product-area names exactly as listed above. Retain **Aralytics**. The `Talas` folder name and historical proposal references may remain for repository compatibility and project history; do not show Talas in new or rebranded UI. Do not rename database tables, migrations, routes, or stored identifiers as part of the visual rebrand.
