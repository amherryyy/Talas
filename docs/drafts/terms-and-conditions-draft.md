## 1. About Duno

Duno is a browser-based study organization tool. It provides student-owned subject and topic lists, rule-based study priorities, study plans, a timer with an explicit save step, descriptive Aralytics summaries, general study suggestions, and optional private workload and energy check-ins. These features support a student's own organization; they do not certify attendance, assess health, predict grades, or guarantee academic results.

The service requires an internet connection for account pages and saved study data. The offline page and selected public design files do not make account pages or changes available offline.

## 2. Accounts and acceptable use

Users must provide an email address they are authorized to use, keep authentication details private, and use Duno only for lawful personal study organization. Users must not attempt to access another person's records, disrupt the service, submit unlawful content, or misuse the service or hosting resources.

The current public registration flow creates student accounts. Administrative access is separately provisioned and is not an ordinary registration choice. Users are responsible for the accuracy of academic information they enter and may update their own supported records within the app's rules.

## 3. Student records and privacy

Subjects, topics, plans, study sessions, and optional check-ins are associated with the signed-in account. Students must not enter information about another person unless they have permission and the institution has approved that use. The current product does not request free-text check-in notes.

The separate **Privacy Notice draft** describes known app behavior and unresolved processing details. It must be approved and published before these terms are offered for acceptance. A privacy notice is not consent to optional processing.

## 4. Service availability and records

Duno may be unavailable during hosting, network, or maintenance problems. Students should verify important schedules independently. The app does not promise uninterrupted access, automatic backups, or recovery of deleted records; the owner must confirm actual provider and backup practices before publication.

The app currently does not provide a self-service account deletion or data export flow. The owner must establish and publish a working support/request process before these terms are used. Do not infer that deleting a subject deletes its related history: the app may block deletion where saved study records depend on it.
