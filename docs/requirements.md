# Talas 2.0 Phase 1 requirements

**Status:** Project-owner-approved MVP scope baseline as of 2026-10-13, reconstructed from `docs/development-blueprint.md`. The owner reports that no separate Phase 2 rubric was issued and a live demo is required. The source proposal is not present in this repository. Detailed product rules listed below remain undecided and must be decided before the affected behavior is implemented.

## Product goal and boundary

Build a responsive, browser-based MVP in one month that helps a student organize subjects and topics, choose what to work on, plan available study time, record completed sessions, and review descriptive summaries. Optional workload or energy check-ins remain private and are not health assessments.

The MVP does not include AI-generated recommendations, grade prediction, reminders, calendar integration, class or group accounts, or native apps. Priority, plan, summary, and study suggestion outputs must be deterministic and explainable. Do not make claims about learning outcomes or grades.

## Functional requirements and acceptance criteria

| ID | Priority | Requirement / acceptance criteria | Security and privacy acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F-01 | Must | The public start page introduces Duno and links visitors to student registration or sign-in. A student can register through open student registration, verify the email address with a one-time numeric code, then sign in and sign out using the approved session-based authentication flow. Unverified accounts cannot access student features. A pending signup can be discarded to correct a mistyped email; abandoned pending signups are removed after 48 hours when registration is opened. Public registration always creates a student account; users cannot choose or submit a role. | Use CodeIgniter Shield APIs; enforce CSRF on state-changing forms, safe session settings, and rate limiting on registration, code delivery/resend, and code verification. Codes expire after 15 minutes, allow at most five failed submissions, have a 60-second resend cooldown and a five-send daily cap per account, and remain subject to Shield's 10 requests/minute per-IP auth limit. Never store plaintext passwords. |
| F-02 | Must | The app distinguishes student and admin access; student-only routes reject admins where inappropriate, and admin routes reject students. | Deny by default on protected routes and authorize on the server for every request. Hiding a link is not authorization. |
| F-03 | Must | A student can create, view, edit, and delete their own subjects. | Scope every query and mutation to the authenticated owner; a guessed or foreign subject ID must not reveal or alter another student's data. |
| F-04 | Must | A student can create and manage topics under their subjects, with due date, importance, and progress fields. | Verify that the parent subject belongs to the current student on every read and write. Validate all fields server-side and escape output. |
| F-05 | Must | A student can search and filter their topics/subjects using the available fields. | Search results and filter counts contain only the current student's records; use parameterized queries. |
| F-06 | Must | The student sees a rule-based priority order with a short explanation based on entered deadline, importance, or progress. Empty and overdue cases are handled. | Output only the student's records. Rules and tie behavior are documented and tested; no unsupported prediction or opaque AI ranking. Exact formula remains a product decision. |
| F-07 | Must | A student can enter 15–480 available minutes and receive one 25-minute block per unfinished topic in F-06 order, shortening the last block to fit. They can edit durations, remove and reorder blocks, and save one plan per local date; replacing a plan requires confirmation. | Validate duration and referenced records on the server. Store and display only within the student's owner scope. See [`docs/plan-generation-story.md`](plan-generation-story.md). |
| F-08 | Must | A student can time a study session for a subject and optional topic, review it, and explicitly save it. An interrupted or refreshed timer never silently creates a session. Students can edit or delete saved sessions. | Browser timing is not trusted. Validate 1–480 whole minutes and ownership/relationship of subject and optional topic on the server; protect writes with CSRF. See [`docs/study-session-story.md`](study-session-story.md). |
| F-09 | Must | A student can view simple Aralytics summaries of study activity, see a separate private rule-based pattern count and rating distribution chart from their check-ins, and print a readable study report. | Study totals and print data are owner-scoped. Keep private check-in patterns on the signed-in student's screen only; omit them from printed reports and admin data. Escape rendered values and provide an accessible numeric equivalent to the chart. |
| F-10 | Should | The app presents brief curated study suggestions as supporting content. | Suggestions are static/curated and do not claim guaranteed outcomes, diagnose, or infer personal traits. No generative AI. |
| F-11 | Should | A student may optionally record a private workload or energy check-in and see descriptive rating counts for the last 7 days and the selected Aralytics period. | Keep entries and pattern summaries private to that student. Exclude them from admin aggregates, search, print, and exports. Collect the minimum needed and document retention/deletion behavior before implementation.
| F-12 | Should | An admin can view aggregate totals for student accounts, subjects, topics, saved study sessions, and study minutes. | Show totals only. Exclude admin-group accounts and all account identities, record details, check-in values/patterns, exports, and drill-down. Admin access remains server-authorized. See [`docs/admin-dashboard-story.md`](admin-dashboard-story.md).
| F-13 | Must | The main experience works on mobile and desktop, supports keyboard use, provides a readable print layout, and gives new students a skippable, replayable guide to the existing tools. Existing role navigation and brand links provide Home access. Use the page's existing destination-specific Back or Cancel link on contextual detail/form flows rather than adding a duplicate shell button. Error pages provide recovery navigation. | Remember tour completion per student in the current browser only. Do not put private data in URLs, client logs, or publicly accessible assets. |
| F-14 | Should | A student earns 1 XP per saved study minute and 5 XP for the first authenticated student visit on each app-local calendar day. Consecutive visit days build a streak; a missed day resets it on the next visit. Levels unlock additional deterministic Aralytics views while core Aralytics stays available. | Award each student/date once, scope activity rows to that student, exclude check-in values, and keep progression out of admin aggregates and printable reports. Study-time badges and study-day charts count saved sessions only. No grades, predictions, or external runtime services. See [`study-progression-story.md`](study-progression-story.md). |

## Cross-cutting security requirements

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S-01 | Authentication and authorization use framework facilities and server-side checks. | Feature tests cover signed-out access, role boundaries, and direct requests to protected routes. |
| S-02 | Every record read/write checks authenticated ownership, including nested topic/session references. This is application-enforced row-level access control: MySQL/MariaDB on the target host does not provide native row-level security policies. | Cross-user tests use two dummy students and verify no read, update, delete, count, export, or relation attach crosses owners. |
| S-03 | All inputs are validated server-side; all user-provided output is escaped; database access is parameterized. | Tests cover invalid values and rendered payloads; review confirms query safety. |
| S-04 | State-changing requests require CSRF protection; sessions use secure settings appropriate to HTTPS deployment. | Framework configuration review plus feature checks; staging HTTPS smoke check. |
| S-05 | Secrets and real student data never enter source control, demo data, logs, or CI. | Repository secret/data review; use synthetic seed data only. |
| S-06 | Private application paths and configuration are inaccessible over HTTP. | Staging direct-URL probes to `.env`, `app`, `writable`, `vendor`/`system`, configuration, and backup files return 403/404. |
| S-07 | Optional check-ins are minimized and private by default. | Tests verify student owner scope and absence from admin aggregate, search, and print/export paths. |
| S-08 | Errors in production do not disclose stack traces, secrets, or study data. | Production-mode configuration review and safe error-path smoke check. |
| S-09 | Responses include baseline browser security headers, and production database errors do not show SQL details. | Inspect response headers and confirm production `DBDebug` is disabled; verify safe error handling on the deployed host. |
| S-10 | Dynamic application pages are not stored by browser or shared caches. | Verify `Cache-Control: no-store, max-age=0, no-cache` on public, auth, student, and admin responses; versioned public assets may use their documented static cache policy. |

## Confirmed product decisions

- **F-06 priority formula (2026-10-15):** Only unfinished topics are scored; importance contributes low/medium/high = 1/2/3; due date contributes overdue/today = 3, within 7 days = 2, later = 1, undated = 0; not started adds 1 and in progress 0. Sort by score, nearest due date, then title and show the reasons. See [`docs/priority-story.md`](priority-story.md).
- **F-07 daily plan (2026-10-16):** The owner approved 15–480 available minutes, one 25-minute block per unfinished topic in F-06 order with the final block shortened to fit, editable/removable/reorderable blocks capped by available time, and one saved plan per local date with confirmation before replacement. If no unfinished topics exist, the saved plan has no blocks. See [`docs/plan-generation-story.md`](plan-generation-story.md).
- **F-08 study sessions (2026-10-16):** The owner approved whole-minute durations from 1–480, explicit saving after timer review, and editing/deleting saved sessions. An interrupted timer must never silently save. See [`docs/study-session-story.md`](study-session-story.md).
- **F-08 subject-history deletion rule (2026-10-16):** The owner decided to block subject deletion while any saved study session references that subject, preserving the saved history. See [`docs/study-session-story.md`](study-session-story.md).
- **Timer rhythm guidance (2026-10-25):** The owner requested an emphasized timer card during active study and optional, fixed focus/break suggestions. The Timer may suggest 25/5 Pomodoro or 50/10 longer blocks; the student pauses the elapsed timer for breaks, and only running time is counted. This guidance does not trigger countdowns or save anything automatically. See [`docs/ui-ux-redesign-story.md`](ui-ux-redesign-story.md).
- **Dashboard check-in prompt (2026-10-25):** The owner requested a deliberate pop-up asking about study energy and how demanding or stressful studying feels. Show it at most once per student per app-local day if they have not checked in yet, make “Not now” available, and save ratings only after explicit submission. It continues to use the approved private workload and energy check-in record. See [`private-check-in-story.md`](private-check-in-story.md).
- **F-14 study progression (2026-10-25, extended 2026-10-26):** The owner chose to keep the existing core Aralytics open and unlock optional extras through XP. Saved study time earns one XP per minute. The owner approved an additional five XP for the first authenticated student visit on each app-local calendar day; consecutive days increase the streak, and a missed day resets it on the next visit. Check-ins do not earn XP. Study-time badges and study-day charts still derive only from saved sessions. See [`study-progression-story.md`](study-progression-story.md).
- **F-09 Aralytics summaries (2026-10-16, amended 2026-10-17):** The owner approved total minutes, session count, average length, and subject totals for today, 7 days, 30 days, and all time. The printable report includes the selected period, totals, subject/topic breakdown, and dated session list. Study totals and print continue to exclude check-ins; a separate rule-based pattern count appears on the student's screen only under the F-09/F-11 amendment. Periods use application-local calendar dates. See [`docs/aralytics-story.md`](aralytics-story.md).
- **F-10 curated study suggestions (2026-10-16):** The owner approved four fixed, general study suggestions adapted from the What Works Clearinghouse practice guide, shown as cards on the student dashboard with source links. Suggestions are not personalized and make no guaranteed outcome, diagnosis, or personal-trait claim; no generative AI is used. See [`docs/study-suggestions-story.md`](study-suggestions-story.md).
- **F-11 private check-ins (2026-10-17):** The owner approved workload and energy ratings from 1 to 5, multiple entries per day, edit/delete, and retention until the student deletes an entry. Entries remain student-only; no individual values are shown in admin views, search, or reports. No free-text note or automatic deletion is included. See [`docs/private-check-in-story.md`](private-check-in-story.md).
- **F-11 weekly reflection summary (2026-10-17):** The owner selected a private summary for the last 7 application-local calendar days. Count entries where workload is 4–5 and energy is 1–2; describe only the student's recorded ratings and do not change the study plan. See [`docs/private-check-in-story.md`](private-check-in-story.md).
- **F-09/F-11 private rule-based patterns (2026-10-17):** The owner approved showing a separate, student-only summary of high-workload and low-energy counts plus a paired rating-distribution bar chart in Aralytics for its selected period. This is deterministic counting of self-reported entries, not model training or a prediction. Omit the pattern and chart from print, admin, search, and exports; provide equivalent numeric counts for accessibility. See [`docs/aralytics-story.md`](aralytics-story.md) and [`docs/private-check-in-story.md`](private-check-in-story.md).
- **F-12 admin dashboard (2026-10-17):** The owner approved five aggregate cards for student account count, subject count, topic count, saved study session count, and total saved study minutes. Exclude admin-group accounts, identities, individual records, check-ins, exports, and drill-down. See [`docs/admin-dashboard-story.md`](admin-dashboard-story.md).
- **F-04 topic management (2026-10-15):** Required title up to 120 characters; duplicate titles allowed per subject; optional date-only due date with past dates allowed; importance is low/medium/high; progress is not started/in progress/done; new topics default to medium importance and not started progress, and both values can be changed later. Topic deletion is blocked while saved study sessions reference it, with a restrictive database foreign key as the future race-safe backstop. See [`docs/topic-management-story.md`](topic-management-story.md).
- **F-05 search and filter (2026-10-15):** Search subject names and topic titles/parent subject names; filter topics by subject, importance, progress, and due-date state. Search forms use CSRF-protected POST requests to keep search terms out of URLs. Overdue means before the current app date and not done; upcoming means today or later and not done; undated means no due date. See [`docs/search-filter-story.md`](search-filter-story.md).
- **F-01 public start page (2026-10-19):** The owner asked to replace the CodeIgniter starter page with a Duno-branded public start page, using their mobile app screenshots as visual inspiration. Use a responsive, rounded tool grid and student bottom navigation while keeping the content tied to existing Duno features. Visitors can register or sign in; signed-in students and admins get links to their role-appropriate workspaces. Describe only approved product features and keep optional check-ins private. See [`docs/auth-foundation-story.md`](auth-foundation-story.md).
- **F-13 mobile navigation (2026-10-19; guest mapping approved 2026-10-25):** The owner asked for a consistent fixed bottom navigation bar on the public start page and signed-in app pages at mobile widths, with the current section marked. The bar uses Duno's approved palette; desktop navigation remains at the top, and secondary student pages stay available in a compact hamburger dropdown on mobile. For guests, the bar uses Home, Tools, Guide, and Join: the first three jump to public sections, Join opens student registration, and sign-in remains in the top menu.
- **F-13 first-visit guide (2026-10-26):** The owner requested a suggestive tour on a student's first app visit, with a skip option, and chose browser-only completion storage. Explain the existing Home, Focus, Plan, Timer, Aralytics, Guide, and More pages; keep the tour available from More after it is skipped or finished. See [`student-onboarding-story.md`](student-onboarding-story.md).
- **Duno mascot (2026-10-19):** The owner supplied a brand board and approved the lightbulb wearing a Filipino salakot as the interim app mascot. A static transparent mascot appears on the public welcome page and student dashboard. The board remains the visual reference; production vector art and additional expressions can be supplied later.
